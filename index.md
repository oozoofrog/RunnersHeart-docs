Heart Rate Zone Running App for Apple Watch & iPhone

## Features

- Real-time heart rate monitoring with zone feedback
- Haptic alerts when entering new heart rate zones
- GPS route recording with Kalman filter smoothing
- Edwards TRIMP training load calculation
- Automatic resting heart rate estimation
- Beautiful workout summaries with shareable cards
